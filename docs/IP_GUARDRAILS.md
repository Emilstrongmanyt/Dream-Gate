# IP guardrails

Kindling is original IP. Names, fiction, presentation, captains, units, and UI
chrome must not copy Blizzard / Hearthstone / Battlegrounds.

## Ban list (CI)

`client/` and `content/` must not contain:

- `Bob`
- `Tavern`
- `Battlegrounds`
- `Hearthstone`

This document and `docs/DESIGN.md` may mention the banned terms for legal
clarity. Store copy and in-game strings must not.

## Presentation

Dusk marketplace, not an inn. No lookalike innkeeper, card frames, fonts, or
sound-alikes. Internal “mechanically inspired by 8-player auto-battlers” notes
stay out of store copy and the client.
